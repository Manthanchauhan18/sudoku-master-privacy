Privacy Policy

Sudoku Master (package: com.sudokumaster.app) · Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Sudoku Master app (“the app”) uses. We keep it simple: the app has no accounts, no sign-in and does not ask for your name, email or location.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress, statistics, achievements, daily-challenge history and settings (theme, sound, haptics, mistake limit) are stored only on your device. We do not receive or upload them. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. App backup is turned off.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. This is handled by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, on most Android phones).

3. Firebase

The app uses Google Firebase (Realtime Database) to download its ad settings and update information. This request contains no personal information from you, but, like any internet request, it exposes your IP address to Google. See the Firebase privacy information.

4. Permissions

The app uses the internet (to load ads and settings) and vibration (haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings). It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. The only third parties involved are Google (AdMob and Firebase), as described above.

6. Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

All network requests made by the app use HTTPS. Data we keep lives on your device only.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
